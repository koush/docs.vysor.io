# Vysor Pro

Vysor can be used for free for screen mirroring and control of Android and iOS devices, including screenshots. Vysor Pro unlocks the remaining features.

## Free vs Pro

| Feature | Free | Pro |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Mirror and control Android and iOS | ✅ | ✅ |
| [Screenshots](/manual/screenshots) | ✅ | ✅ |

| [Vysor Share](/share) |  | ✅ |
| [AI automation (MCP)](/manual/mcp) |  | ✅ |

Desktop notifications, drag-and-drop file transfer, and multiple displays are Android-only. AI automation (MCP) is available in the native desktop app while a device viewer is open.

[Enterprise](/enterprise) adds team licensing and web-based Share access management.

## Purchasing

Log in to Vysor and click **Upgrade** to purchase Vysor Pro. Licenses are per-account and can be used on all of your devices. <!-- TODO(verify): purchase options (monthly/annual/lifetime) per the approved pricing before publishing. -->

## Managing Your License

Open the [Vysor billing portal](https://billing.v6.vysor.io/) and choose **Manage existing** for an individual subscription or **Manage Enterprise** for a team.

If you have purchased a license in the past, log in with the same account and Vysor will recover it automatically.
