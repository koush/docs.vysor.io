# Vysor Pro

Vysor Pro unlocks higher quality mirroring and additional features.

## Features

- **High Quality Mirroring.** Increased bitrate and resolution for a sharp, low-latency mirroring experience. When High Quality Mirroring is enabled, you will be prompted to keep it for each device you connect.
- **Fullscreen.** Mirror your device fullscreen.
- **Wireless.** Connect to Android devices over the network, no cable required.
- **Desktop Notifications.** Mirror your Android device's notifications onto your desktop. Notifications arrive alongside the mirrored device with click-through actions.
- **All Displays.** View and switch between every display of a multi-display Android device.
- **Screenshots and Recordings.** Capture screenshots and record your device screen.

## Purchasing

Log in to Vysor with your Google or Apple account and click **Upgrade** to purchase Vysor Pro. Licenses are per-account and can be used on all of your devices.

## Managing Your License

Vysor Pro licenses are provided by a few different sources. Your license type is shown at the bottom of the device list, with a link to manage it:

- **Stripe/PayPal (Individual License):** managed at [billing.clockworkmod.com](https://billing.clockworkmod.com)
- **Enterprise:** managed at [billing.vysor.io](https://billing.vysor.io)
- **Chrome Web Store:** managed at [Google Payment subscriptions](https://payments.google.com/payments/home#subscriptionsAndServices)

If you have purchased a license in the past, log in with the same account and Vysor will recover it automatically.
