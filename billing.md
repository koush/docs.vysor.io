# Billing

## Refunds

First time purchases are refundable within 24 hours of purchase. Contact [billing@vysor.io](mailto:billing@vysor.io) from your purchasing email account to request a refund. Include the account email used for the purchase, or an order/receipt number.

Subscription renewals are non-refundable. Automatic subscription renewal can be disabled at any time, and the subscription will remain active for the remainder of the term.

## Managing and Canceling Subscriptions

Subscriptions are managed by the billing service used for the purchase. Your license source is shown at the bottom of the device list, with a link to its management page:

- **Stripe/PayPal (Individual License):** [billing.clockworkmod.com](https://billing.clockworkmod.com)
- **Enterprise:** [billing.vysor.io](https://billing.vysor.io)

Invoices and receipts are available on the same management pages.
