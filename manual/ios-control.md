# Controlling iOS

Vysor injects mouse and keyboard input over USB through Apple's CoreDevice HID interface. Control is built into the desktop app; no companion app or Accessibility configuration is required.

See [iOS Setup](/ios) for requirements, including enabling Developer Mode, and first-time setup.

## Mouse and Touch

- Click to tap the corresponding point on the device.
- Click and drag to drag on the device.
- Scroll with the mouse wheel to swipe vertically.
- Input is injected directly as touch events.

## Keyboard

Type with your computer keyboard. Text and keyboard shortcuts are sent to the device as HID key events.
