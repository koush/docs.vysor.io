# Wireless Connections

Vysor can switch a USB-connected Android device to a wireless connection:

1. Connect the device with a USB cable.
2. Press the **WiFi icon** next to the device in the device list.
3. The device window opens over the network — you may disconnect the cable.

The device and computer must be on the same network for the wireless connection to work.
