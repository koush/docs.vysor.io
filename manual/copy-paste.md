# Copy and Paste

**Computer to Android:** copy text on your computer, then press `Ctrl-V` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd-V` (Mac) while the Vysor device window is focused to paste it into the device.

**Android to computer:** copy text on Android using the device's text selection tool, then paste on your computer.
