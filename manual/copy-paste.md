# Copy and Paste

Copy and paste works between your computer and Android and iOS devices.

**Computer to device:** copy text on your computer, then press `Ctrl-V` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd-V` (Mac) while the Vysor device window is focused to paste it into the device.

**Device to computer:** copy text on the device with its text selection tool. The text is pushed to your computer's clipboard automatically, ready to paste.

**Typing non-English characters (Android):** enable the **International Keyboard** setting ([Window Settings](/manual/customize-window#window-settings)). This uses the input method from the [Vysor Android app](https://github.com/koush/docs.vysor.io/releases/latest/download/Vysor-release.apk) to send characters from your desktop keyboard directly, and hides the soft keyboard on the device.
