# Multitouch

Vysor can simulate a second finger for multitouch gestures (pinch to zoom, rotations, and so on):

1. Hold `Alt` on the keyboard and click the mouse to place the first "finger".
2. Use the mouse to touch and move the second finger.
3. `Alt` + mouse click again to release the first finger.

Demo: [Multitouch](https://twitter.com/vysorapp/status/1259224506188460032)
