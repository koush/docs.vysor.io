# Screenshots

1. Click **Take Screenshot** (the camera button) in the device window [title bar](/manual/customize-window#title-bar).
2. By default Vysor shows a preview with **Copy to Clipboard** and **Save** buttons. Saving downloads a `screenshot-<timestamp>.png` to your computer.

To skip the preview, choose what happens when a screenshot is taken under **Window Settings → When screenshot is taken** in the [device settings](/manual/customize-window#settings): show a preview, save to a file, or copy straight to the clipboard.

Screenshots work on Android and iOS devices alike.
