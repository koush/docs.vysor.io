# Window Settings

The Vysor device window has several settings found in the device window menus:

## Always On Top

Keep the Vysor device window on top of other windows.

## Navigation Bar

If your Android device already has a built-in navigation bar, the one Vysor draws can be removed with this setting. (The navigation bar itself can also be customized — see [Customizing Vysor](/manual/customize).)

## Pin Title Bar

Toggle this to make the title bar stay pinned, or to have it disappear when not in use.
