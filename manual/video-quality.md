# Video Quality

Vysor ships with several preset video qualities. Try the presets to see what works best for your Android device, then adjust to your preference.

Notes:

- Higher bitrates and resolutions can result in higher latency and lag.
- Different USB ports can result in different performance. Counterintuitively, USB 2.0 ports often have **lower latency** than USB 3.0 ports (USB 3+ ports can sometimes be identified by their red or blue coloring).
